About Me

Designer by craft,
builder by habit.

4 reasons to hire me:

1.

Fast without cutting corners. I move quickly while keeping the quality high — and I'm always leveling up, eager to learn from the people around me

2.

Full ownership you can trust. I take complete ownership of everything I work on — self-directed and dependable, the kind of hand-off you never have to chase.

3.

Complexity into measurable impact. I untangle complex challenges and drive them all the way to real, measurable results — asking the right questions to get there.

4.

A people person and ego-free team player. Strong communicator, open to feedback, and focused on the product winning — not on being right.

Bottom line: I bring real results and good energy to every team — the kind of teammate you actually want in the room.
