About Me

Designer by craft,
builder by habit.

I'm a Senior Product Designer with roots in visual design and branding, and deep expertise in design systems. I care - sometimes obsessively - about the details and specs that make a product feel right, without ever losing sight of the bigger picture or the user behind it.

I'm at my best untangling complex UX challenges and turning them into something clean, elegant, and genuinely usable. I'm a true team player: I communicate openly, actively welcome feedback, and check my ego at the door - because what matters to me is the success of the product, not who gets it right. I move fast without cutting corners, take real ownership, and bring calm, positive energy to every team I'm on.

Off the clock, you'll find me with my pets Oliver, Oscar and Gustav in Emek Hefer, doing sports, or with the people I love. And if that sounds like a cliché - I've got the recommendations to back it up. 🌱
